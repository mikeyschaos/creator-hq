# Creator HQ

> **Stay Consistent.**

Creator HQ is an open-source dashboard built for hobby content creators.

Whether you create videos after work, on weekends, or whenever inspiration strikes, Creator HQ gives you a clean, always-on dashboard that helps you stay consistent without overwhelming you with analytics.

Creator HQ is designed for Raspberry Pi but can run anywhere Flask is supported.

---

## Features

- Live YouTube statistics
- Upload health monitoring
- Subscriber growth tracking
- Progress toward monetization
- Creator tips
- Recent wins
- Expandable channel cards
- Browser-based dashboard
- Browser-based admin panel (coming soon)

---

## Philosophy

Creator HQ follows four design principles.

### One Glance Rule

You should understand the health of your creator business in under ten seconds.

### Stay Consistent

Small, consistent actions beat perfection.

### Celebrate Progress

Celebrate wins just as often as reminders.

### Keep It Simple

Every widget must earn its place.
